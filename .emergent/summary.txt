**original_problem_statement:**
The user's objective is to build a comprehensive ticketing platform named D-Billet (or Ticket Djibouti). The project has evolved significantly from its initial scope.

**LATEST PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
The platform must support three distinct user roles: **Customer**, **Organizer**, and **Admin**.

*   **Organizer Dashboard:** A self-service portal for event creators to manage their events. Key features include:
    *   KPIs for sales and revenue, and a sales performance graph.
    *   Management of events, ticket types (e.g., VIP, Standard), and promotional codes.
    *   A searchable guestlist with an option to export to CSV/Excel.
    *   A finance section to view earnings and request payouts via D-Money, Waafi, or bank transfer.

*   **Admin Dashboard:** A complete control panel for the platform owner to oversee all operations. Key features include:
    *   A global dashboard with platform-wide KPIs (total revenue, platform commission) and urgent alerts (e.g., pending payouts).
    *   Full management of events created on the platform, including the ability to approve, block, or feature them on the homepage.
    *   User management, with the ability to view all users, manage organizers, and set custom commission rates.
    *   Financial oversight, including a log of all transactions and an interface to approve or reject organizer payout requests.
    *   Platform-wide configuration for settings like commission rates, event categories, and static content (Terms of Use).
    *   Exclusive management of the Train and Ferry transport services, including schedules, routes, and pricing.
